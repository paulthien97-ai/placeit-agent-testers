# Brief: Place It (cozy packing puzzle for kids and families, offline)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal changes, because this is a game with no account.

- The app is a drag-and-drop puzzle: drag items from the tray into a suitcase or
  container until everything fits. Between levels there is a home yard with a pet
  (Bot), a farm, a sky garden and a shop.
- Out of scope: real payments and ads. This is a debug build with test ads. Never
  complete a purchase: you may open a buy dialog to read it, then cancel. If a test
  ad shows, close it. Decline sign-in to Google Play Games.
- The game keeps text at its own size on purpose: ignore your persona's system font
  size; judge layout as shown.
- Ignore the account part of your persona's goal. Your goal instead, as your persona:
  1. Finish the first 3 levels (follow the on-screen hand and tips).
  2. Open Bot's Home (yard). Open the Decor tray: switch its two tabs (Decor / Farm),
     open its shop, look at the "Special 💎" group and the "Yard background" row.
  3. Tap the fish pond and read its panel. Go up to the sky garden and open the pot shop.
  4. Open the main shop: the Gems, Coins and Premium tabs.
- Report especially: steps that block or confuse a new player, tutorial bubbles that
  cover what they talk about or point off screen, text left in Vietnamese or English
  in your language, labels cut off or overlapping, buttons that do nothing, panels you
  cannot close, and anything that looks broken on your screen size.
