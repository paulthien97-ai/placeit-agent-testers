# Brief: Place It, deeper pass (cozy packing puzzle for kids and families, offline)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal changes, because this is a game with no account.

- The app is a drag-and-drop puzzle: drag items from the tray into a suitcase or
  container until everything fits. Between levels there is a home yard with a pet
  (Bot), a farm, a sky garden, a shop and seasonal events.
- Out of scope: real payments and ads. This is a debug build with test ads. Never
  complete a purchase with real money: you may open a buy dialog, then cancel. Spending
  in-game coins is fine. If a test ad shows, close it. Decline Google Play Games sign-in.
- The game keeps text at its own size on purpose: ignore system font size.
- Ignore the account part of your persona's goal. Your goal instead, as your persona,
  skip the parts you already know and go further than the first levels:
  1. Play levels 2 to 6. Try the different kinds of levels the map offers (shelves,
     belts, pairs...). Use Undo, Hint and Reset at least once.
  2. Open the Journey / level map and scroll it. Open the daily missions and the
     calendar or event panel if one is shown.
  3. In Bot's Home: open the farm area, tap an animal, a coop and a field; open the
     task book (both tabs); feed or play with Bot.
  4. Go up to the sky garden: plant and water a pot if possible.
  5. Open Settings: change the language to another one and back, toggle music and
     sound, open "How to play", then close Settings.
- Report especially: anything that blocks or confuses, panels you cannot close,
  buttons that do nothing, text cut off or overlapping, text not in your language,
  wrong plurals or missing spaces, images missing, and anything slow or frozen.
